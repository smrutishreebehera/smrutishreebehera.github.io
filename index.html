01 / About

A physicist.
A curious mind.

Smrutishree Behera
Doctoral researcher in Physics

My research begins with the behaviour of materials—and a curiosity about what they could make possible.

I am pursuing a PhD in Physics at SRM University-AP, India, working on ferroelectric materials for neuromorphic device applications. My academic foundation spans condensed matter physics, solid-state physics, quantum mechanics and experimental characterization.

Alongside my doctoral work, I am developing a research interest in ferroelectric-enhanced neurotechnology and neural modulation. I am interested in how functional materials might contribute to future neural interfaces and brain-inspired devices.
